# Mitsuketa

## Design Context

### Users
Public-facing NZ company ownership investigation tool (search by name/NZBN/NZCN, explore ownership graphs). Anybody can use it; the FBU internal team are also daily users. Assume no training — the interface must be self-explanatory.

### Brand Personality
Intuitive, professional, quietly Japanese. Part of a family with the creator's other apps (Mihari, Insolvency Insights) — references to be supplied; keep the thematic thread.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Japanese motifs carried by restraint, not decoration. Approved brand moment: the hanko seal imprint — vermilion seal-red (#e04e39) on ink-navy, 見つけた / MITSUKETA wordmark.
- Light and dark are BOTH first-class. Design every surface in both from the start.
- Anti-references: the current Google AI Studio default look (stock Tailwind blue, gradient clip-text wordmark, emoji-as-UI, system-font-only), and generic AI dashboard slop.
- Intro load animation prototype (approved): https://claude.ai/code/artifact/413f8177-379a-4a01-93f5-da051d638452

### Design Principles
1. Function over form — features and flows are settled before visual treatment is applied.
2. The graph is the product; nodes, edges, and status colors get the most design investment.
3. One token sheet: single neutral ramp, one radius system, one accent (seal red), documented status ramp.
4. Identifiers (NZBN/NZCN) are core content — tabular numerals, easy copy, high legibility at small sizes.
5. Accessible by default: visible focus, aria-labels on icon buttons, AA contrast, real touch targets.
