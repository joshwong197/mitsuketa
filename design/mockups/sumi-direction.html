Why this works

Direction one made Mitsuketa a well-behaved sibling of Mihari. This direction gives it its own temperament: Mihari watches, Mitsuketa finds. Every choice below traces back to that.

墨Ink depth is information

Sumi painting grades its ink formally: full strength, half, pale wash. Here the grade encodes hops from your target. The searched entity is pressed in full ink at 2px; each ring outward dilutes. You read investigative distance at a glance, before you read a single name.

染The brand wears its own dye

Mihari owns the red hanko. Mitsuketa's seal takes a dye of its own, and red is released to mean danger and nothing else, so a liquidation flag is the only red thing on the canvas. Three candidate dyes, switchable live in the header:

藍 Ai · indigoaizome, the working dye. Recommended.
松 Matsu · pinesteady, forestry-NZ adjacent
芥 Karashi · mustardwarm, archival, riskiest

筆The graph draws itself

Edges are brush lines that draw in when a branch expands, exactly like the approved intro where the ownership web spreads from the stamp. One CSS animation on stroke-dashoffset, GPU-cheap, gone entirely under reduced motion. Motion only ever means "new evidence arrived".

Hit "Redraw ink" on the canvas to replay it.

紙Washi, not slate

The ground is paper: a warm-tinted neutral with a static grain layer, one fixed element with zero repaint cost. Dark mode is the ink-stone version of the same sheet, not an inverted blue-black. Registry work is paperwork; the surface says so quietly.

簿A case file, not a sidebar

The panel stops being generic chrome. It is the case: the entity under investigation, counts that matter (entities, depth, flags), and a timestamped trail of what you did. The trail doubles as your audit record, which is what an investigator actually wants to export.

印What survives from direction one

The stamp on the target, inkan rings for people, the 紅琥青 severity squares. Those were the strongest ideas and they stay, re-dyed. The family resemblance to Mihari now lives in these shared marks and the shared severity language, not in a shared palette.
