Typeset as a register, not assembled as a dashboard

You were right that the last one still read as generated, and the tell is nameable. I had built every section the same way — a bordered card with an uppercase mono label on top — which is the generic documentation pattern, repeated six times. Mitsuketa's identity is a document tradition. This is the report rebuilt as one. Nothing is committed; this is for your call.

What changed

The tells I removed

  • Six bordered cards stacked at identical width and weight, so nothing led.
  • An uppercase mono eyebrow over every one — the single loudest generated-doc tic, and I used it everywhere.
  • A three-column stat grid and a row of pill buttons, both straight out of dashboard boilerplate.
  • Badges on every row, which spent the emphasis budget evenly and therefore spent it on nothing.

What replaces them

  • One spine rule down the full page with kanji section markers hanging in the margin — the register's own vocabulary: 登記 register, 所有 ownership, 現況 current state, 履歴 history, 負担 burdens.
  • Ruled ledger rows instead of boxes. Labels right-aligned against a hairline, the way a printed spec table sets.
  • A double rule under the masthead and year rules knocked out across the spine — printing conventions, not borders.
  • One stamp on the page: the status hanko, rotated, carrying the only shadow the sumi spec allows. Boldness spent in a single place.
  • Filters became a ruled line of words, matching the app's own mode line.

I excluded standing burdens rather than whitelisting mortgages and caveats, so a live lease on a commercial title still surfaces, while easements and covenants drop to 負担 below in full. Readability was the constraint, so two things are deliberate: body text stays at 13–13.5px gothic with commentary capped near 64 characters, and mono is now reserved for identifiers and dates only — previously it carried every section label, which flattened the hierarchy. Kanji are decoration in the margin, never load-bearing: every section also carries its English name.
